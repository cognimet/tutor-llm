# Everything AI Tutor

An AI learning companion that helps every student learn better, understand deeper, and never fall behind.

## Why it helps

- **Personalized to every learner** — Lessons adapt to each student's pace and level, so no one is left behind and no one is held back.
- **Finds and fixes learning gaps** — It spots exactly what a student is struggling with and re-teaches those weak spots until they truly understand.
- **Learns until mastery, not just completion** — Instead of moving on too soon, it keeps guiding until the concept is genuinely mastered.
- **Available anytime, patient every time** — Students get instant, judgment-free help whenever they need it, as many times as they need it.
- **Keeps parents in the loop** — Parents get clear insight into progress, strengths, and areas that need attention.
- **Makes learning engaging** — Interactive, conversational, and encouraging — designed to keep students curious and motivated.
- **Affordable, transparent access** — Families stay in control of usage and cost, making quality tutoring accessible to more students.

## Who it's for

- **Students** — a supportive tutor that meets them where they are.
- **Parents** — clarity and confidence in their child's progress.
- **Educators & institutions** — a scalable way to give every learner personal attention.
